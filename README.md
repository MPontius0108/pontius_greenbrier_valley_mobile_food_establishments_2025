## Acquiring and Normalizing the Data
There are several datasets available via the U.S. Census Bureau, USDA, Bureau of Labor Statistics, and Data.gov for general demographic and food consumption information. I utilized webscraping from data aggregates such as Google Places, Google Analytics, and Facebook.com to collect data on food establishments in the Greenbrier Valley to obtain a sense of the food consumption and restaurant environment on a local level. 


## Problems and Hurdles
My biggest issue was trying to find food truck market data specifically for the region of Greenbrier Valley. There were few if any dedicated sources specialized in that area. I was able to aggregate data via webscraping from local web pages that helped inform my analysis.

## Technologies Used
